## Findings

1. **Upload screen still offers adding to an existing deck.** It is titled
   "Add a lecture" and its "Into" picker lists existing decks, which contradicts
   "a new deck is the way to add material". *Fix: always a new deck; title
   "New deck"; remove the picker.*
2. **Job screens show the filename, not the deck name.** Planning and plan review
   show the whole PowerPoint title in the header, even though the user typed
   "Antibiotics" (the generating screen already shows the deck name).
   *Fix: prefer `deck_name` in every job-screen header.*
3. **The deck screen's due count is stale after studying.** It showed "Study 146
   due" after two cards were rated; Today showed 144. It only corrected itself when
   the deck was reopened. *Fix: reload the deck when the screen regains focus.*
4. **"due for review" right after studying (minor).** After one Good rating,
   Beta-Lactams reads "due for review" at 5%. The label fires on low mastery, not
   on due cards, so it reads as wrong just after a review.
5. **"1 topics" (cosmetic).** The deck subtitle doesn't singularise.
6. **Lesson back arrow goes back one step (question).** Leaving a lesson from a
   later step takes a swipe from the left edge. The back arrow only pages
   backwards, and the top-right icon is a topic switcher. Worth deciding whether
   the arrow should exit.

Not a bug: the plan estimated ~123 cards and generation produced 146. It is an
estimate, but a 19% overshoot is worth watching against the cost estimate.
